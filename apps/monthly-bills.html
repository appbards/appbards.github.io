    Monthly Bills: Bill Tracker

    Get it onGoogle Play

    Know which bills you've paid and what is still left to pay. Monthly Bills: Bill Tracker brings your recurring bills, due dates and payment checklist together in one simple monthly view.

    Add rent, utilities, internet, subscriptions and other regular expenses. Check off each bill after you pay it and keep track of what still needs your attention. No account or bank connection is required.

    A SIMPLE MONTHLY BILL CHECKLIST

    Want to organise your bills without setting up a full budget? Keep a clear list of your monthly expenses, with amounts, categories and payment status. Mark a bill paid and it is crossed off, just like a paper checklist.

    SEE WHAT IS STILL UNPAID

    Check your remaining total and how many bills you've paid this month. Move completed bills to the bottom so unpaid bills stay easy to find. Bills without an amount are excluded from totals until you enter their cost.

    RECURRING BILLS, READY EACH MONTH

    Set regular expenses to repeat every month. Your recurring bills carry over automatically, even if you haven't opened the app. The latest known amount is ready to edit, so you don't have to rebuild your list each month. Add one-off expenses alongside your regular bills.

    TRACK BILLS BEFORE THE AMOUNT ARRIVES

    Electricity and other utility bills can change from month to month. Add a bill with its amount left blank, then enter the final cost when it arrives. Update recurring amounts whenever needed.

    DUE DATES AND BILL REMINDERS

    Add an optional due day and choose a reminder:

    • On the due date
    • One day before
    • One week before

    Reminders stop once you mark the bill paid.

    ORGANISE AND REVIEW YOUR EXPENSES

    Create categories, choose their colours and add notes to your bills. See your expenses by category and review previous months to check your payment history.

    QUICK ACCESS AND BACKUP

    • See how much is left to pay with a home-screen widget.
    • Export your bills, categories and history to a backup file, then restore them when needed.
    • Choose your currency and use light, dark or system theme.

    COMMON QUESTIONS

    Do I need to connect my bank?

    No. Enter bills manually and mark them paid yourself. Monthly Bills tracks payments; it does not pay bills or read your bank transactions.

    Can I use it without internet?

    Yes. Add bills, mark them paid and receive reminders offline. The app contains ads and uses online services for advertising, analytics and diagnostics.

    Is it a full budgeting app?

    Monthly Bills focuses on your monthly bill list, recurring expenses and payment reminders. It is for people who want a straightforward bill organiser.

    Start with the bills you pay every month. Check them off as you pay and see what is left.
