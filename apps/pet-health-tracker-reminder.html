    What is Pet Health Tracker Reminder?

    Pet Health Tracker Reminder is a pet health tracker for Android and iOS, made by App Bards. It keeps your pets' health records in one place: a profile for each pet, weight history, vaccination records, medications and reminders. Before a vet visit, you can export your pet's information as a PDF.

    It is made for pet owners who would rather keep these details on their phone than on paper cards, in notes and in old messages, whether they have one dog or cat or a household with several animals.

    Pet profiles and health records

    Create a separate profile for each dog, cat or other pet, with a photo, name, species, breed, gender and birth date. You can also save the microchip number and any allergies, so these details are at hand when a vet, groomer or pet sitter asks for them.

    Add as many pets as you have. Each pet keeps its own health records, so weights, vaccinations and medications are never mixed up between animals.

    Pet weight tracking

    Record your pet's weight whenever you weigh them and add a note to an entry, for example after a vet visit or a change of food. Every entry is saved in a weight history you can look back through, in metric or US units.

    With the in-app purchase, the pet weight tracker also shows the changes on a chart. This makes it a handy cat weight tracker for a growing kitten, or a way to follow a dog's weight between check-ups. The app only records the numbers; your vet can tell you what they mean for your pet.

    Vaccination records and reminders

    Save each vaccination with its name, the date it was given and its expiration date. The app keeps the vaccination history for every pet and shows which vaccinations are coming up, so dog vaccination records and cat vaccines are easy to check before a vet appointment, a trip or a stay at a boarding kennel.

    Set pet vaccine reminders for upcoming doses and get a notification on your phone. Vaccination records and reminders are both free.

    Medication tracking and reminders

    The pet medication tracker records each medication with its dosage, how often it is given, and its start and end dates. It suits short treatments as well as ongoing ones, and pet medication reminders let you know when a dose is due.

    Medication management is part of the in-app purchase. It is for keeping track of what your vet has prescribed: the app does not recommend medications or doses.

    PDF export for vet visits

    Create a PDF with your pet's information to take to a veterinary appointment, or to share with a new vet, a pet sitter or a boarding kennel. The PDF includes:

    • Profile details, such as breed, birth date, microchip number and allergies
    • Vaccination records with expiration dates
    • Current medications
    • Recent weight history

    PDF export is part of the in-app purchase.

    Simple, private and offline

    • No account or sign-in. Open the app and add your first pet.
    • Works offline. Your pet records are stored on your device and are not synced to an online account.
    • No ads.
    • Backup and restore, so you can save a copy of your pet records and restore it later.

    In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. The App Bards privacy policy explains how data is handled.

    Free and paid features

    Pet Health Tracker Reminder is free to download. The free version includes:

    • Pet profiles for all your pets
    • Weight entries and weight history
    • Vaccination records
    • Vaccination reminders

    An in-app purchase adds:

    • Weight charts
    • Medication management and medication reminders
    • Pet information PDF export

    The in-app purchase is available as a subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Current prices are shown in the app and on Google Play and the App Store.

    Common questions

    Can I keep my dog's vaccination records in the app?

    Yes. Save each vaccination with the date it was given and its expiration date, look back through the vaccination history and set reminders for upcoming doses. This is free.

    Can I track my cat's weight?

    Yes. Weight entries and weight history are free, in metric or US units. Weight charts are part of the in-app purchase.

    Does it support medication reminders?

    Yes. With the in-app purchase you can record medications, dosages and schedules, and get reminders when a dose is due.

    Can I export my pet's information for the vet?

    Yes. The PDF export, part of the in-app purchase, creates a document with your pet's profile, vaccinations with expiration dates, current medications and recent weight history.

    Do I need an account?

    No. There is no sign-up or login.

    Does it work without an internet connection?

    Yes. Your pet records are stored on your device, so you can add and view them offline.

    Is it available on Android and iOS?

    Yes. Download it from Google Play for Android or from the App Store for iPhone.

    Is it a medical or diagnostic app?

    No. It is for personal record keeping. It does not diagnose health problems or give veterinary advice.
