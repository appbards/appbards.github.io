    Pet Health Tracker Reminder

    Download on theApp StoreGet it onGoogle Play

    Pet Health Tracker Reminder helps you organize essential care records for every animal in your family. With our practical pet helper, you can create separate profiles, follow weight changes, save vaccination details, manage medications, and set useful reminders.

    PROFILES FOR EVERY PET

    Add any type of animal and create an individual profile with a photo, name, species, breed, gender, birth date, microchip number and allergies. The flexible pet management system keeps each profile organized and easy to update. It’s great for households with dogs and cats, or any other pet, since you can keep every animal’s information separate.

    If you prefer a straightforward pet tracker, you can review essential records without complicated setup or unnecessary features.

    WEIGHT HISTORY AND CHARTS

    Pet Health Tracker Reminder lets you record measurements whenever you want and follow changes on a clear chart. Each pet weight entry becomes part of an easy-to-review history. Use cat health tracking to maintain a consistent record as your beloved feline grows. Even if you own multiple cats, it works as a convenient cat weight tracker with metric and US units available in settings. The chart helps you follow your pet growth and recognize long-term changes more easily.

    This makes everyday pet health monitoring clearer and more convenient.

    VACCINATION RECORDS AND REMINDERS

    Save every pet vaccine with its name, date received, and expiration date. Set a vaccine reminder for an upcoming dose so you know when it is due. Keep a simple dog vaccination record that is always easy to review.

    The pet vaccine tracker helps you to clearly separate previous vaccinations from planned ones.

    MEDICATIONS AND THERAPIES

    With Pet Health Tracker Reminder, you can add current medications, dosage information, treatment frequency, and start or end dates. Our dog medicine tracker helps you stay organized during short treatments, daily medication schedules, and long-term therapies.

    This flexible pet reminder app helps you stay on top of things easily when it comes to taking care of your beloved pets!

    SIMPLE CARE FOR DIFFERENT ANIMALS

    Originally designed for a dog and cat household, but suitable for many other kinds of animals, the app adapts to the companions you care for. Use our dog care tracker for everyday routines and you’ll never miss a feed again!

    Combine profiles, weight history, vaccination details, and medications with one useful dog health care app! However, for everyday feline routines it turns into a perfect cat health app.

    A clean pet care tracker experience makes adding and reviewing information quick and simple.

    ORGANIZED AND PRIVATE

    The simple profile setup gives you a focused cat care app without an overwhelming interface. Another great thing about our pet tracker app is that it keeps records on your own device, available whenever you need them.

    Pet Health Tracker Reminder brings all the important info about your pets into one clear and convenient place.

    Important info: Please note that this app is intended for personal organization and record keeping. It is not a medical or diagnostic tool.
