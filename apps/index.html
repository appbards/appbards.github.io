    Apps by App Bards

    App Bards is a mobile app studio. These are the apps we have published for Android and iOS. Each one has its own page with details and store links.

    Hear My Baby Heartbeat Monitor

    Hear My Baby Heartbeat Monitor is a free Android app that uses your phone's microphone to listen for and record your baby's heartbeat sounds during pregnancy. You can save a recording as a keepsake and share it with family and friends. It needs no doppler or other accessory, and it is not a medical device.

    Square Photo No Crop Profile

    Square Photo No Crop Profile is an Android app that turns any photo into a square picture without cropping it. Fill the space around the photo with a blurred background or one of the included patterns, then use the result as a profile picture or share it on social networks and with friends and family.

    Discover More

    My Baby Story & Stickers Maker

    My Baby Story & Stickers Maker is an iOS app for making stickers and stories from your baby's photos. Cut your baby out of a photo to create a custom sticker, then add backgrounds and other stickers to build stories for birth announcements, monthly milestones and special occasions. Stickers can be shared with family and friends through any image-sharing app.

    Discover More

    Tuning Fork

    Tuning Fork is an Android app that plays a reference tone for musicians and singers. It uses a sine wave tone generator and lets you set the frequency and how long the tone plays. Open the app and press Play, or shake your device to start the tone.

    Discover More

    Pet Health Tracker Reminder

    Pet Health Tracker Reminder is an app for Android and iOS that keeps health records for your dogs, cats and other pets. Each pet gets a profile with a photo, breed, birth date, microchip number and allergies. Log weight, save vaccinations with expiration dates and set vaccine reminders for free. An in-app purchase adds weight charts, medication tracking with reminders and a PDF export for vet visits. No account is needed, records are stored on your device, and the app is not a medical or diagnostic tool.

    Discover More

    Monthly Bills: Bill Tracker

    Monthly Bills: Bill Tracker is an Android app that brings your recurring bills, due dates and payment checklist together in one monthly view. Add bills with amounts, categories and notes, mark each one paid and see the remaining total. Recurring bills carry over every month, an amount can be left blank until the bill arrives, and an optional reminder can be set for the due date, one day before or one week before. No account or bank connection is required.
